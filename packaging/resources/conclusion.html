Installed

If your printer was on the network, the queue Brother_DCP_T420W is ready. Print a test page:

lp -d Brother_DCP_T420W /path/to/file.pdf

If no printer was found, add it in System Settings › Printers & Scanners and pick Brother DCP-T420W (PWG Raster) as the model.

Scanning

brscan                              # full page, colour, PDF
brscan --mode lineart --out doc.pdf # crisp black & white text
brscan --crop a6 --out photo.jpg
brscan --help

Note: this printer's firmware ignores scan settings sent to it — it always scans the full glass in colour. brscan applies the mode, crop, rotation and format afterwards on your Mac.
