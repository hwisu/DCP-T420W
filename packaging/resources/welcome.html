Brother DCP-T420W driver

Printing and scanning for the Brother DCP-T420W, without Brother's software.

This installs:

  • rastertobrother — a CUPS filter that converts print jobs to PWG Raster, the format this printer actually speaks.
  • A PPD — paper sizes, margins, media types and quality modes, generated from the printer's own capabilities.
  • brscan — a command-line scanner client, installed to /usr/local/bin.

If a DCP-T420W is found on your network, a print queue named Brother_DCP_T420W is created for you.

Everything is a native universal binary. Nothing else is required — no Python, no SANE, no background agents.
